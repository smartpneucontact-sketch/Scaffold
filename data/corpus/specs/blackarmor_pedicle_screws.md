# BlackArmor® Pedicle Screw System — Device Description

**Synthetic demo document. Modeled on icotec's public product line; not an actual icotec specification.**

## Part 1 — General

### 1.01 Summary
The BlackArmor pedicle screw system is a posterior spinal fixation system manufactured from carbon-fiber reinforced PEEK (CFR-PEEK) composite. Components include polyaxial pedicle screws, monoaxial pedicle screws, longitudinal connecting rods, set screws, and instrumentation. Radiolucent to X-ray, CT, and MRI.

### 1.02 Material
Composite: continuous carbon fiber reinforced polyetheretherketone (CFR-PEEK), proprietary layup ("BlackArmor"). Tantalum radiopaque markers integrated at distal tip and shaft midline for fluoroscopic visualization during placement.

## Part 2 — Components

### 2.01 Pedicle Screws
- Diameters: 4.5, 5.0, 5.5, 6.0, 6.5, 7.0, 7.5 mm
- Lengths: 25, 30, 35, 40, 45, 50, 55, 60 mm
- Types: monoaxial, polyaxial (40° conical motion), reduction (extended tabs)

### 2.02 Connecting Rods
- Diameter: 5.5 mm
- Lengths: 40, 60, 80, 100, 120, 150, 180, 240, 300, 360 mm
- Material: CFR-PEEK, pre-contoured straight and sagittal-curved options

### 2.03 Set Screws
- Material: Ti-6Al-4V ELI (titanium, the only non-radiolucent component in the construct).
- Torque: 8.0 Nm final, 4.0 Nm provisional.

## Part 3 — Performance

### 3.01 Imaging Compatibility
- X-ray: radiolucent except tantalum markers and set screws.
- CT: no streak artifact from rods or screws.
- MRI: 1.5T and 3T compatible per ASTM F2503; no artifact, no displacement force, minimal heating (< 2°C @ 4 W/kg SAR).
- Radiation therapy: CFR-PEEK has near-tissue-equivalent attenuation; suitable for use in radiated fields.

### 3.02 Mechanical
Per ASTM F1717 vertebrectomy testing: static compression bending, dynamic fatigue, and torsion meet or exceed equivalent titanium constructs.
