# Case 003 — Trauma with Concomitant TBI

**Date:** 2026-04-02  |  **Site:** Level 1 Trauma Center (synthetic)
**Surgeon:** Dr. R. Sato

## Patient
28F. Motor vehicle accident. T12 burst fracture (AOSpine A4) with anterior column collapse 50%, retropulsion 30% canal compromise, kyphotic deformity. Concomitant moderate TBI requiring serial MRI surveillance for evolving cerebral contusions.

## Plan
Posterior instrumented fusion T10-L2, indirect decompression via ligamentotaxis.

## Implants Used
- BlackArmor pedicle screws 6.5 × 45 mm bilateral T10, T11, L1, L2 (8 screws).
- T12 not instrumented (fracture level).
- BlackArmor CFR-PEEK rods 5.5 × 100 mm.

## Why BlackArmor
The serial MRI requirement for TBI surveillance drove the radiolucent choice. Titanium artifact would have degraded T1/T2/SWI sequences sufficient to miss small contusion progression. Trauma surgeon and neurosurgical service jointly selected BlackArmor.

## Outcome
- Serial brain MRI at days 3, 7, 14: clean imaging, contusion stable.
- Spine: union at 6 months. CT shows acceptable kyphosis correction (corrected from 28° to 8°).
