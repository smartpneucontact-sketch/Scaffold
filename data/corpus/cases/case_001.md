# Case 001 — Spine Oncology (Metastatic Breast)

**Date:** 2026-02-14  |  **Site:** Boston Spine Center (synthetic)
**Surgeon:** Dr. M. Khoury  |  **icotec rep:** A. Patel

## Patient
54F, ECOG 1. Hormone-receptor positive metastatic breast carcinoma with new symptomatic compression fracture at T10. MRI shows posterior element involvement T9–T11. Planned 30 Gy in 10 fractions radiation post-fixation. Life expectancy > 12 months.

## Plan
Posterior instrumented fusion T8–T12 with decompression at T10. Goals: mechanical stabilization and preservation of imaging quality for radiation planning and tumor surveillance.

## Implants Used
- BlackArmor pedicle screws 6.0 × 45 mm bilateral T8, T9, T11, T12 (8 screws total).
- T10 pedicle preserved for radiation port — no instrumentation at T10.
- BlackArmor CFR-PEEK rods, 5.5 mm × 120 mm bilateral.
- Titanium set screws (×8) — only metal in construct.

## Outcome
- Radiation planning began at 3 weeks post-op. No artifact on planning CT; target volume contoured cleanly.
- 6-month MRI surveillance: no streak artifact; clear visualization of T10 surgical bed and adjacent levels.
- Patient ambulatory, ECOG 0 at 6 months.

## Note
This case is the prototypical strong indication for BlackArmor: post-op radiation + serial MRI + adjacent-level tumor risk.
