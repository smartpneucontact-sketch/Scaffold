# Case 002 — Adult Degenerative L4-S1

**Date:** 2026-03-08  |  **Site:** Mid-Atlantic Spine Group (synthetic)
**Surgeon:** Dr. J. Lim

## Patient
62M, BMI 28. Long-standing low back pain with L5 radiculopathy. MRI: L4-5 and L5-S1 multilevel DDD with grade I L4-5 spondylolisthesis and L5-S1 foraminal stenosis. Failed 12 months of conservative care.

## Plan
L4-S1 posterior instrumented fusion + bilateral L4-5 / L5-S1 TLIF.

## Implants Used
- BlackArmor pedicle screws 7.0 × 50 mm bilateral L4, L5; 7.0 × 45 mm bilateral S1.
- BlackArmor CFR-PEEK rods 5.5 × 80 mm.
- TLIF cages: non-icotec (per surgeon preference).

## Imaging Benefit Assessment
**Medium.** No oncology / no radiation planned. However, L4-S1 fusion patients have a known ~10% adjacent-segment disease rate at 5–10 years; clean MRI surveillance benefits long-term follow-up. Surgeon preference also cited absence of titanium artifact on follow-up CT to assess fusion mass.

## Outcome
Successful fusion at 12 months on dynamic radiographs. CT at 18 months shows clear visualization of fusion mass without screw-shaft artifact.
