# BlackArmor® Pedicle Screw System — Surgical Technique Summary

**Synthetic demo document.**

## Part 1 — Preoperative Planning

### 1.01 Imaging
Pre-operative CT or MRI of the affected levels. For oncology cases, baseline MRI is recommended before instrumentation; tantalum markers will not obscure post-op imaging.

### 1.02 Templating
Diameter and length selected from preoperative imaging. Typical adult thoracolumbar pedicle accepts a 5.5–7.0 mm diameter screw at 40–50 mm length; T6–T10 typically 5.0–6.0 mm × 35–45 mm.

## Part 2 — Placement

### 2.01 Approach
Standard open posterior midline approach or percutaneous Wiltse-style approach with cannulated technique. Pedicle entry point per surgeon preference (Magerl, intersection of facet and transverse process bisector).

### 2.02 K-Wire Insertion
Place K-wire under fluoroscopic guidance through pedicle into vertebral body. CFR-PEEK is radiolucent — visualize using the tantalum markers built into the screw shaft and tip.

### 2.03 Tap and Insertion
- Probe and tap pedicle as for titanium technique.
- Insert pedicle screw over K-wire. CFR-PEEK screws should not be over-torqued during insertion; manufacturer recommends final torque of 8.0 Nm at set screw, not at screw shaft.
- Polyaxial heads allow 40° of motion; align with rod trajectory before tightening.

### 2.04 Rod Placement
Select pre-contoured rod or contour BlackArmor rod intraoperatively. **CFR-PEEK rods may be in-situ bent within ±15°; bending beyond ±15° is not permitted and may compromise structural integrity.**

### 2.05 Tightening
Insert set screws. Provisional torque 4.0 Nm; final 8.0 Nm. Use icotec torque-limiting driver — over-torque can crack CFR-PEEK threads.

## Part 3 — Imaging Confirmation

Final fluoroscopy AP and lateral. The tantalum markers at distal tip and midline make trajectory and depth visible despite the radiolucent shaft. Final CT or MRI may be obtained without metal artifact obscuring screw placement assessment.

## Part 4 — Cement Augmentation

For severe osteoporosis (T-score ≤ -2.5), cement augmentation is permitted through fenestrated cannulated BlackArmor screws using standard PMMA cement per ASTM F451. Maximum 2 mL per pedicle.
