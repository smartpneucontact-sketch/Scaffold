# BlackArmor® Pedicle Screw System — Indications for Use

**Synthetic demo document.**

## Indications

The BlackArmor Pedicle Screw System is intended for posterior, non-cervical pedicle screw fixation in skeletally mature patients as an adjunct to fusion. Specific indications include:

1. **Degenerative disc disease** (DDD), defined as back pain of discogenic origin with degeneration of the disc confirmed by patient history and radiographic studies.
2. **Spondylolisthesis** (Grades 1–4).
3. **Trauma** (i.e., fracture or dislocation) of the thoracic, lumbar, or sacral spine.
4. **Spinal stenosis** with confirmed instability.
5. **Spinal tumor** (primary or metastatic) — *the BlackArmor system is particularly indicated when post-operative imaging (MRI / CT) or radiation therapy planning would be compromised by metal artifact from a titanium construct.*
6. **Pseudarthrosis** and **failed previous fusion** in skeletally mature patients.
7. **Deformity** including scoliosis, kyphosis, lordosis correction.

## Contraindications

- Active systemic infection or infection localized to the site of implantation.
- Patients with mental or neuromuscular disorders unable to follow postoperative care instructions.
- Pregnant patients (relative).
- Cervical spine fixation (system not indicated for cervical use).
- Skeletally immature patients (relative — see pediatric appendix).
- Known sensitivity to component materials.

## Notable Off-Label Situations Requiring Specialist Review

- **Cervical spine**: not indicated. Refer to icotec cervical product portfolio.
- **Stand-alone (no fusion) use**: not indicated. Always adjunct to fusion.
- **Pediatric patients < 12 yr**: not indicated absent specialist review.
- **Use with non-BlackArmor rods**: not validated. Compatibility limited to BlackArmor CFR-PEEK rods.

## When BlackArmor's Imaging Benefit Is Strongest

- Patients with primary or metastatic spinal tumors who will undergo post-operative radiation therapy.
- Patients with primary spinal tumors requiring serial MRI surveillance (e.g., post-resection follow-up of chordoma, ependymoma, schwannoma).
- Trauma patients with concomitant CNS injury requiring serial MRI.
