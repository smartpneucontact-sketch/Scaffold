# Regulatory & Quality Summary — BlackArmor® Pedicle Screw System

**Synthetic demo document.**

## Clearances & Certifications

- **FDA 510(k)** clearance for posterior, non-cervical pedicle screw fixation (synthetic demo K-number).
- **EU MDR** CE-mark under Class III.
- **Health Canada** licensed.
- **TGA Australia** included on ARTG.
- **PMDA Japan** approved for thoracic, lumbar, sacral.

## Quality System

- ISO 13485:2016 certified design and manufacturing.
- 21 CFR Part 820 compliant.
- UDI (Unique Device Identifier) on every implant per FDA UDI Rule and EU UDI requirements.

## Post-Market Surveillance & Vigilance

### Adverse Event Reporting Thresholds
- **Death or serious injury**: report to FDA via MedWatch within **10 calendar days**.
- **Malfunction without injury but with potential for harm if recurring**: report within **30 calendar days**.
- **EU**: MIR (Manufacturer Incident Report) per MDR Article 87 — same 10/30 day timelines.

### Field Safety Corrective Actions
Initiated only with input from icotec's Post-Market Surveillance team, regulatory affairs, and quality. Notifying Body informed within MDR-defined windows.

## Labeling & Marketing Limits

- **No off-label promotion.** Any claim outside the IFU requires regulatory clearance.
- **Imaging-benefit claims** (e.g. "no artifact on MRI") must be supported by ASTM F2503 testing and 510(k) language.
- **Comparative claims** vs. titanium constructs require head-to-head data on file with Regulatory Affairs.

## Tracking

UDI scan at:
1. Receipt at hospital (logged in inventory).
2. Pre-implantation (logged in OR record).
3. Implantation (logged in patient record + UDI-DI/PI reported to icotec).
4. Explant or replacement (logged via field complaint process).

Data flows to icotec's UDI database, post-market surveillance dashboard, and to FDA's GUDID for the DI portion.
